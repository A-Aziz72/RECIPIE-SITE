Nihari Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 1 lb beef or lamb, cut into chunks
  • 2 tbsp ghee or oil
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 tbsp nihari masala
  • 1/2 cup onions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste

Instructions:

  1. In a large pot, heat ghee or oil and add sliced onions. Cook until golden brown.
  2. Add the meat and cook until it's well-cooked.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and nihari masala. Mix well.
  4. Add yogurt and cook for another 10 minutes on low heat.
