Biryani Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 1 cup meat (chicken or lamb)
  • 2 tbsp biryani masala
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 1/2 cup onions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp ghee or oil

Instructions:

  1. Cook the rice separately until it's half-cooked.
  2. In a large pan, heat ghee or oil and add sliced onions. Cook until golden brown.
  3. Add the meat and cook until it's well-cooked.
  4. Add biryani masala and yogurt to the meat. Mix well.
  5. Add the half-cooked rice to the meat mixture and mix gently.
  6. Cook for another 5-10 minutes on low heat, allowing flavors to blend.
