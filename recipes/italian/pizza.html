Pizza Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 1 pizza dough
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 1 cup mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup pepperoni slices
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the oven to 475°F (245°C).
  2. Knead the pizza dough on a floured surface until smooth.
  3. Roll out the dough into a circle and place it on a baking sheet.
  4. Sprinkle olive oil over the dough.
  5. Spread tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  6. Add mozzarella cheese and pepperoni slices on top.
  7. Bake for 12-15 minutes or until the crust is golden brown and cheese is bubbly.
