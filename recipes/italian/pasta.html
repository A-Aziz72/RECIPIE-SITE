Pasta Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 200g pasta
  • 2 cups tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. Boil water in a large pot, add salt, and cook pasta according to package instructions.
  2. In a pan, heat olive oil over medium heat. Add minced garlic and sauté until fragrant.
  3. Add tomato sauce to the pan, season with salt and pepper, and let it simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Drain the cooked pasta and add it to the sauce. Toss to combine.
  5. Serve hot, garnished with fresh basil or grated cheese if desired.
